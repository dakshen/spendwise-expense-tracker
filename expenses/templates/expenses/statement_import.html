Where do I get these files?
  • PhonePe: open History, tap Download Statement, then choose the months or financial year.
  • Google Pay statement: open your transaction history, tap the More icon, choose Get Statement, pick the period and save the PDF.
  • Google Pay Takeout: export your Google Pay data from Google Takeout and unzip it. Upload the transactions CSV or the My Activity HTML file from inside it, not the .zip.

Your file is processed for extraction. A PDF password, if you enter one, is used only to open the file and is not stored. Review the results before importing. Do not upload a statement on a shared or public computer.
